# Exhaust the Design Space

Explore alternatives when a consequential design choice has no clear precedent and more than one approach could satisfy the requirements.

- Identify the constraints and the dimensions that distinguish the candidates.
- Compare a few genuinely different sketches, prototypes, or designs when their expected learning is worth the effort.
- Evaluate them against the same user needs, operational constraints, and maintenance cost.
- Record why the selected option fits best.

Do not prototype by default for established patterns, mechanical edits, clear bug fixes, or a choice dictated by constraints. Explicitly approved designs and supplied references are constraints; compare only unresolved choices unless concrete evidence exposes a conflict with acceptance or safety. Alternatives can be explored sequentially; parallel work is useful only when its cost and coordination are justified.
