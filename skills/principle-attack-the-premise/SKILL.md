# Attack the Premise

When two or more attempts built on the same assumption fail the same gate, treat that pattern as evidence against the assumption.

1. Write the shared premise in one sentence and identify the failing check.
2. Compare the attempts and inspect evidence that could confirm or disprove the premise.
3. If the issue concerns an imbalance across actors, take a small repeatable census of who holds the role or load.
4. If the evidence disproves the premise, investigate a different cause instead of adding another compensating fix.

Do not build a census tool unless repeating or reviewing the measurement justifies it. Keep investigation and any resulting change within the authorized scope.
