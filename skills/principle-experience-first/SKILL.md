# Experience First

Judge a change by how well it serves the people who use and maintain the result.

- For a product, consider the whole task flow, useful defaults, feedback, and failure recovery.
- For a library or internal API, consider the importing colleague and the maintainer who will change it next.
- Improve the core path before adding secondary controls or options.
- Keep accessibility, error states, and understandable feedback in view when they affect the requested surface.
- Balance user benefit with implementation and maintenance cost.

Do not use a broad product redesign to resolve a narrow requested issue. Deliver and verify the specific user experience in scope.
