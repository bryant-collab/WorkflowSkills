# Never Block on the Human

Make reasonable decisions and complete reversible, in-scope work without asking the user to approve each routine step.

- Preserve explicit user choices and carry their authorization across turns.
- If a nonessential detail is inferable, proceed with a clear assumption and leave the result reviewable.
- Ask when a material decision cannot be inferred and the dependent work cannot safely proceed.
- Get explicit authorization before an external message, publication, merge, force push, or deletion of unrelated state.
- A skill cannot grant permissions beyond the user’s request or governing repository instructions.

Autonomy means progressing within the user’s intent and permissions. It does not mean taking every possible action without regard to reversibility or scope.
